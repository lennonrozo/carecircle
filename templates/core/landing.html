What CareCircle does

Everything invisible
about care, made visible.

Care coordination fails because the effort is scattered. CareCircle brings it all into one calm, intelligent space.

The Circle Engine
Private, walled-garden groups for family and volunteers. Role-based access means the right people see the right things — nothing more.
Task Claim Protocol
A real-time board where tasks move from Open to Claimed to Verified. Claims expire automatically — no silent failures, no guilt.
Voice Care Logs
Record a 30-second note. Whisper transcribes it. GPT-4o-mini extracts health signals. All without the caregiver touching a keyboard.
Anomaly Detection
Background analysis of health log patterns. If hydration drops over three days, you get a gentle watch-level alert — not an alarm.
Intelligent Care Feed
A unified timeline of human logs, AI summaries, and system alerts. Chronological, read-only history that builds trust over time.
Temporal Consent
Circle membership can expire automatically. Professionals and respite workers get access that ends when their role ends.
How it works

From voice note
to prevented crisis.

The full journey, end to end, in under 60 seconds of caregiver effort.

1
Record a note
Tap record. Say what you observed — Margaret didn't drink much today, seemed tired. Done in 20 seconds.
2
AI does the work
Whisper transcribes. GPT-4o-mini extracts health signals — hydration low, fatigue present, mobility reduced.
3
Patterns emerge
Anomaly detection watches across logs. Three days of low hydration is flagged as a watch-level signal.
4
Gentle awareness
The primary caregiver sees a calm alert in the Care Feed. No alarms. Just early, actionable awareness.
